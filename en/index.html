How to use

FlyQRCode helps you create scannable QR codes in seconds — no install, no sign-up. Enter your content and preview updates on the right. Download when you're ready for print or sharing.

Quick start

  1. Paste a URL, plain text, or WiFi string into QR content;
  2. Pick a preset or customize colors, dot shape, and size;
  3. Optionally add a center logo for branding;
  4. Click Download PNG or Download SVG.

Common uses

  • Marketing — landing pages on posters and flyers;
  • Events — check-in, giveaways, resource links;
  • Guest WiFi — connect without typing passwords;
  • Digital cards — contact info or social profiles.

Design tips

For print, use the Minimal B&W preset with enough quiet zone (margin). For screens, try gradients and rounded dots. Test with a real phone before going live.
