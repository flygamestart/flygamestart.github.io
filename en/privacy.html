Welcome to FlyQRCode (“we”, “this site”). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the site you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Content you enter

Text and images you use to generate QR codes are processed locally in your browser by default. History is stored in your device’s local storage (localStorage).

1.2 Technical data

Our host and third-party services (e.g. Google AdSense) may collect browser type, visit time, approximate location from IP, referrer, and device information for analytics, security, and advertising.

1.3 Cookies

We use cookies and local storage for consent choices and history. With your consent, ad partners may also use cookies.

2. Google advertising (AdSense)

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other sites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and partners to serve ads based on visits to this and/or other sites.
  • Opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and improve the QR generator;
  • Show ads to support free operation (with consent where required);
  • Analyze usage and prevent abuse;
  • Comply with law and platform policies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with ad/analytics providers or when required by law.

5. Retention

You can clear on-device history via the site. Server logs follow our providers’ default retention.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region you may access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing, and withdraw cookie consent.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe we received child data.

8. International visitors
