QR Codes with a Center Logo: Design Tips

Balance branding and scan reliability

Why add a logo?

A center logo strengthens branding on posters, packaging, and cards. FlyQRCode supports uploads and preset icons. Logos cover data modules — size and contrast matter.

Design rules

  • Logo area ~30–38% of the code; reduce if scans fail;
  • Square transparent PNG works best;
  • Strong contrast between dots and background;
  • Default ECC Q (~25%) suits most logo use cases.

Workflow

  1. Generate without logo first;
  2. Add a small logo and test each change;
  3. Export PNG for digital, SVG for print.

Common failures

Oversized logo, low contrast, print too small (<2 cm), or blurry camera — fix these before mass printing.
