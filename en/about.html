Who we are

FlyQRCode is a free online QR code generator for individuals and creators. Enter a link, text, or WiFi config, preview instantly, and download PNG or SVG for print and social sharing.

Features

  • No sign-up — open the page and start creating.
  • Custom styles — presets, dot shapes, colors, and optional center logo.
  • Local processing — your content is generated in the browser, not uploaded.
  • History — recent items saved on this device for quick re-download.

Use cases

Website promotion, event check-in, product docs, community invites, digital business cards, in-store WiFi, and more.

Acceptable use

Please create lawful QR content only. Do not use this tool for fraud, infringement, or illegal material. See our Terms of Use.

Ads

To keep the service free, we may show ads via Google AdSense. Ad content is provided by third parties. See the Privacy Policy for cookie and data details.
