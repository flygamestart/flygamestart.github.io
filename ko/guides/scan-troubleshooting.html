Recognition vs redirect

No camera reaction usually means graphic quality issues. Recognition with a failed open usually means wrong content or a dead link.

Content issues

  • Missing https:// or typos;
  • Invalid WiFi string;
  • URL too long — use a short link;
  • Intranet-only links off-network.

Style issues

  • Low contrast; heavy gradients;
  • Logo too large;
  • Overly decorative dot shapes on older phones.

Try minimal black & white, remove logo, increase size.

Environment

Too small, glare, dirt, bad lighting, wrong distance — all affect scans.

Steps

  1. Verify content in preview;
  2. Switch to high-contrast style;
  3. Increase size and margin;
  4. Remove logo and retest;
  5. Try another phone / lighting;
  6. Contact us if still stuck.
