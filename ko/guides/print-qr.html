Print vs screen

200px may look fine on a monitor but fail on a small printed sticker. Plan for physical size, quiet zone, and file format.

Recommended sizes

  • Posters: ≥3 cm side, larger if viewed from far away;
  • Flyers / table tents: 2.5–4 cm;
  • Business cards: ≥1.5–2 cm away from text;
  • Curved packaging: go larger.

Margin

Keep margin ≥8 (12+ recommended). Do not place decorative borders against the code.

PNG vs SVG

PNG at 800–1200px for simple workflows; SVG for vector print pipelines.

Pre-press checklist

  1. URL works over https;
  2. Scan succeeds on multiple phones;
  3. Enough margin, logo not too large;
  4. Provide SVG or ≥300dpi PNG to print vendor.
