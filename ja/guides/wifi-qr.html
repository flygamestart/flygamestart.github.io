Why WiFi QR codes?

Cafés, stores, and offices often repeat network names and passwords. A WiFi QR code lets visitors connect with one scan — fewer questions at the counter and a smoother experience.

Standard format

WIFI:T:WPA;S:MyNetwork;P:MyPassword;;
  • T — security: WPA, WEP, or nopass
  • S — SSID (case-sensitive)
  • P — password (omit for open networks)
  • End with ;;

Generate with FlyQRCode

  1. Open the generator and paste the string;
  2. Use high-contrast presets for print;
  3. Size 280–400px, margin ≥ 12;
  4. Download PNG for signs or SVG for print shops.

Testing

Test with iPhone Camera, Android Camera, and your regional super-app scanner before posting. Avoid glossy curved surfaces and strong backlight.

FAQ

No reaction when scanning? Check extra spaces and exact SSID match. Do not publish WiFi QRs on the public internet — use them offline only.
